# 🗂 Project map

| Where | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| `app/pages/index.vue` | The front door, which is the hall of fame: the search in the navigation row, the menu of the site's fourteen rankings, the banner that opens the hall, the five plates about the population, and the breakdown of the population as a whole under them. It draws no table: the rows live in the rankings the menu leads to |
| `app/pages/privacy.vue` | The privacy policy: what is collected, what a sign-in stores, and what the counters are |
| `app/pages/about.vue` | About the site: the story of the project, the six things it does, what it stands on, and where to start |
| `app/pages/legal.vue` | The legal and trademark notice: whose game this is, whose site this is not, and where the data comes from |
| `app/pages/admin.vue` | The admin panel, as the shell it now is: the tab strip in the navigation row, the sign-in gate, the timer that keeps the diagnostics fresh (`app/composables/adminDiagnostics`) and the tags that stand the tabs on screen. Every tab is a file of its own under `app/components/admin/` (`AdminSystemTab`, `AdminScanTab`, `AdminStatisticsTab`, `AdminSeasonTab`, `AdminMergeTab`, `AdminContentTab`, `AdminAccessTab`), so the prose that explains a tab lives with the tab |
| `app/app.vue` | The root: the route announcer, `<NuxtLayout>` around `<NuxtPage>`, the cookie question and the page preloader |
| `app/error.vue` | The page an address that matched nothing is answered with - and the page a render that broke is answered with: the site's own frame and the plate in `app/components/SiteMissing.vue`, which a character the site could not read is given as well |
| `app/pages/[region]/[realm]/[name].vue` | The character shell: header, tabs, share / download, refresh |
| `app/pages/[region]/[realm]/[name]/` | The tabs themselves: `index` (overview), `collections/*`, `achievements/*`, `reputations`, `activity`, `compare/*` (the comparison - `/compare` with nobody to compare against yet, and `/compare/:vsRegion/:vsRealm/:vsName` with a second character) |
| `app/components/` | `AppLegend` (the site's question mark: a round `?` that opens a pane of its own - the score's multipliers and the reputation journal's own reading today - hung at the body and placed by the engine, with the caller's content in the default slot), `AppCollapse` (a fold-out that animates its real height - the comparison board's open rows, the profile main's stat panel, the collections/achievements/reputation legends, the phone's navigation groups and the profile note), `CollectionsGrid`, `CollectionShelf`, `CollectionScopeSwitch` (whose collection the figures are: this character's or the whole account's, on a shelf and on the summary), `CollectionMenu`, `AchievementSummaryGrid`, `AchievementShelfGrid`, `AchievementShelf`, `AchievementMenu`, `ReputationShelf`, `ReputationRow`, `ActivityFeed` (the timeline of the achievements a character earned most recently, under the heading tile it shares with the reputation journal), `ActivityGoldScale` (the bar under that heading: the feed divided into the four kinds of event the rail counts, one shade of gold each), `CharacterRenderBackdrop` (the render fixed behind the overview and the two summaries, with the tiles parting around it), `CharacterName`, `WowheadLink`, `AppIcon`, `SiteNotifications` (the site's notifications: one stack of toasts in the corner of the window, above every page, raised by `useNotifications` from wherever something happened and drawn by the root), `AppSwitch` (the track-and-knob filter every shelf prints), `ProgressBar` (the one bar the summaries and shelves fill), `SummaryTile` (the card the collections, achievements and profile overview build their tiles from - a count, its end, the tier colour, the glyph and the scope note), `ProfileHeadlineTile` (the large score and Mythic+ plates at the head of the profile's two rails), `ShelfLegend` (the filter row every shelf prints and the note behind its "where these numbers come from" button), `TabMenu` (the split tab and caret the character navigation uses), `LocaleSwitch`, `HeaderTools` (the reader's own group in the corner of every header: the language the site is being read in and the account it is read with, as one plate of the site's glass), `SelectedCharacterChip` (the character this browser selected, as one plate of the same glass standing beside that group - a control of two sections, who it is and the press that reads their place in the table the page is drawing and opens it turned to their row - drawn only by a header a page has handed a `ranking`, which is the hall of fame and every ranking page), `ScopeSwitch` (the pair of chips a scope question is asked with, on the collections pages and on the reputation journal), `SiteBackdrop` (the artwork, scrim and vignette every page stands on), `SiteHeader` (the brand, the page's name, and the row under it - the site's own navigation, which this header draws whenever a page has none of its own), `SiteNav` (the site's *only* row: the character's five views under the character's own name, the comparison as the second half of that very entry - the name opens the profile, the half beside it carries the two arrows and opens the comparison - the site's own two entries - the hall of fame (a control in two halves: its tab opens the hub, its caret drops the site's fourteen rankings, `HallOfFameMenu`) and the search - held apart from them by a hairline, the language pair, the account, and the panel a phone opens all of it in; a page that is not about a character wears it with those left out), `SiteFooter`, `SiteDocument` (the frame the about page, the policy and the legal notice are cut from), `SiteMissing` (the page a reader is answered with when what they asked for is not here - the 404, the render that broke, and the character the site could not read: one plate for all three, with the search in it, because a mistyped character is what most of them are), `DocumentNav` (the rail of those documents, beside them), `ProfileStatsNote` (what the account page's figures are and where they come from, folded behind its own title), `ScoreLegend` (the multipliers the site's score is made of, drawn on that question mark (`AppLegend`): a bare `?` wherever the figure it explains stands - beside the gold figure on the profile, beside the field of the score's own ranking, and on the front door's plate about the overall rating), `HallOfFameMenu` (the site's own entry of the navigation row, in two halves the way the collections tab is: the tab opens the hub, and the caret beside it drops the fourteen rankings of the site), `CookieNotice` (the cookie question, and the way back to it), `SiteStatistics` (the admin panel's Statistics tab: four cards for what the site holds now, four SVG tracks over one time axis for how each got there, and the sentences the four read as together), `SupportButton`, `BackToTop`, `MyProfile` (who this browser is signed in as), `CharacterSearchForm` (the two fields that look a character up), `CharacterSearchDialog` (the same two fields, opened from a page's own row), `CharacterVersus` (the comparison board: two characters and their thirteen figures side by side, each row's winner lit), `VersusMetricScale` (how full one collection is, as the bar, the two figures and the share over the charts of an open row - drawn only where a size for that figure is known), `FavoriteStar` (the star that saves a character for the favorites: every row of the hall of fame and a character's own name wear it), `CharacterCardActions` (the pair of buttons a comparison card carries - save the character, read them again - and the moment their figures were read, under them) |
| `app/pages/leaderboards/` | The metric leaderboards: `[metric]` (the world), `[metric]/[region]`, `[metric]/[region]/[realm]` - one page per figure the site ranks, at every scope it ranks it in, each under `/ru` as an alias; each route keeps its page alive across parameter changes by keying on the route's name and reading the address through `computed` |
| `app/layouts/` | `leaderboard` - the frame whose `SiteBackdrop` stays mounted while a metric page changes, so the artwork does not blink between two tables |
| `app/plugins/` | `reveal.ts` (registers `v-reveal` and `v-collapse` on both the server and the browser), `tip.ts` (registers `v-tip`: the tip any element can wear - the plate raised at the body and placed by the engine), `siteStatistics.ts` (starts the recorder of the site's own daily figures), `presence.client.ts` (the presence heartbeat) |
| `app/components/leaderboard/` | `HallOfFameBanner` (the banner the hall opens with: the site's own promise - your story in Azeroth - its two doors (the overall ranking, and the card of the character this browser opened last, or the search that finds one) and the size of the hall - how many characters and realms - over a drawing of the hall itself: three receding arches, a lit floor and a summit, in SVG and in the site's own gold), `LeaderboardStats` (the front door's five plates: who is on the site right now and the leader of mounts, M+, achievements and decor), `LeaderboardBreakdown` (the same aggregate turned on the population itself: the realms the members are spread over - each of them a link into that realm's own ranking - the classes they play, the sides they play for, and everything they have gathered between them, under the day the table was last written), `LeaderboardFilters` (the site's fourteen rankings as one menu - the one being read wearing the gold - and, under a hairline, the filters every page that draws a table wears: which side, which region, which realm and a name, with the legend of the score beside the field; the front door has no table and is told so, so it wears the menu alone - `filters`), `MetricLeaderboard` (a ranking's own page: the table and the way through it; it reads its metric, region and realm from the address so another ranking reuses the same pane, keeps the last rows while the next answer lands, and keeps its glass free of `opacity`), `MetricLeaderboardTable` (`# | Character | Guild | Realm | <the figure>` - the one table of the site, and its rows are the row the plates and the board wear: the place in a medal, the side as a badge beside the name, the portrait with its halo and its online dot, the name with the star and the two badges, the class, the level and the item level, the guild in a column of its own; the pane animates its own height as their number changes and each answer fades in as one block, but no row is animated on its own), `MetricDetailPanel` (one character's opened figure for the account page: the fill bar where the size of the collection is known, the same places, distribution and history blocks the comparison board draws under an open row, for one side rather than two), `MetricRankLinks` (where one character stands - world, region, realm - above the open row's charts: a heading over a row of equal columns, one column per place actually held, so the row never wraps), `MetricRankLink` (one of those places as a chip: the scope over its number, a link into that very ranking already turned to the character's row - the sentence the chip is too small to print stands in its tooltip and its label) |
| `app/composables/` | `characterView` (what the shell hands its views: the profile, the feed, and its own Refresh with its cooldown), `collections`, `collectionView` (the switches a shelf carries: what is shown, and whose collection), `reputationView` (whose journal a page reads: the character's or the account's), `achievements`, `achievementView`, `reputations`, `presence` (the beat and who is on the site), `notifications` (the site's one queue of messages - a toast per event, with its tone's own life, a hold while it is being read and the bar that counts it down), `characterSearch` (whether the character search is out - the one sheet in the navigation row, which the hall's banner opens with a button of its own), `cookieConsent` (the reader's answer and the Consent Mode call it makes), `metricLeaderboard` (a ranking's own address: the slug, the scope, the page a `?rank=` link lands on, and the key its answer is filed under), `realmNames` (the site's own realm list, which a metric page also asks whether a realm exists at all), `lang`, `seo`, `searchHistory` (the characters looked up, and the merge that draws them beside the saved ones), `versusFavorites` (the characters saved with a star, which the board and every search field offer), `savedCharacters` (the bridge to the account's own copies: one read of `/api/saved` per page, the browser's lists handed over once, and the three writes that follow a lookup, a star and a cross), `siteSettings` (the site's own editable settings, read once for the whole site and saved from the panel), `characterRanks` (the places one character holds in every ranking of the site, and the address of a ranking turned to the one they hold in it - what every door from their figures into a table is written from), `characterFigures` (one character's thirteen figures, whose account each is and the denominator a collection is measured against - the one rule the comparison board and the profile's stat panel share), `sectionNavigation` (the sticky rail, the header measurement and the two observers the collections, achievements and reputation shelves share - including the retry a deferred section gets), `spotlight` (the pointer position the tile spotlight paints from), `blockEntrance` (the site's one entrance and list rhythm — `enterAt`, `enterGlass`, `enterSheet`, `enterTableText`, `LIST_MOTION`, `v-reveal`, `v-collapse`), `urls`, `relativeTime`, `wowheadPower`, `adminDiagnostics` (the process's own figures, their poller and the shared state three admin tabs read — the panel starts the timer and every tab reads the answer), `adminFormat` (how the admin panel spells a counted figure, a size, a span and a moment, so its seven tabs cannot disagree) |
| `app/utils/` | `floating` (where a pane hangs: the site's own gap, margin and middleware for `@floating-ui/vue`, read by both `AppLegend` and `v-tip`), `adminRows` (the one row shape the admin panel's detail lists are built from - the System and Season tabs are files of their own and build the same rows) |
| `server/api/` | The endpoints above |
| `server/utils/` | Blizzard client, the atlas and its readers (`collectionAtlas` holds the one shelf counter every collection bar is measured by), the card renderer, the SWR cache, reputations, achievements, the Armoury reader, the character index, `leaderboardStorage` (the table's pages, a character's place, a metric's pages, the places a comparison asks for and every ranking's place for one character), `savedCharacters` (the two lists an account remembers), `siteSettings` (the settings an administrator edits, and who may edit them), `siteStatistics` (the four figures the site counts about itself, one point per day), `achievementFingerprint` (what an account owns in a character's achievement log, and the three anchors read out of it as a signature), `characterFingerprint` (the fingerprints the site holds, what a stored log's signature is, and the hand-out scan), `guestAccounts` (the guests a panel pass reads, the signature groups it draws with their evidence, and the pair figures beside them), `accountMerge` (the merge itself: measuring a group, building the derived account, moving the roster into it, the audit row and the undo), `accountSync` (the roster's own writes: the characters a summary names, and the two that make a sign-in *adopt* the account a merge built for the reader, or empty it into the account they already have), `guestMergeScheduler` (the timer the automatic merge switch arms) |
| `scripts/` | The offline refreshes (see below), the derivation of the fallback typeface's metrics (`npm run derive:font-metrics`, which prints the block in `app/assets/css/main.css`) and `deploy.mjs` |
| `shared/data/collectionsSchema.ts` | The collection types, the section shape, and the Russian label map |
| `shared/data/reputationsSchema.ts` | The reputation journal's types, the stage message keys, and the synthetic group's name |
| `shared/data/leaderboardSchema.ts` | The hall of fame's record, query and payload types, and the fourteen metric leaderboards a reader can open a ranking of - shared by the server and the components |
| `shared/data/accountFingerprint.ts` | `SIGNATURE_ANCHORS` — the three achievements that are an account's exact identity, with the measurement behind them (and the share-based fences they replaced, recorded as the measurement that retired them) |
| `shared/data/guestMerge.ts` | What the merge panel is drawn from: the bounds a group is cut at, the settings an administrator owns (auto-merge, cadence, groups per pass), the shapes the list travels in, and the strict validation of both a settings save and an action body |
| `shared/data/siteSettings.ts` | What an administrator can change, and what a copy of the site ships with: the maxima a figure nobody publishes a size for is measured against, the shape the settings travel in, and the Battletags that open the panel |
| `shared/utils/leaderboardScore.ts` | `calculatePlayerScore` and the weights it is made of |
| `shared/utils/consent.ts` | The cookie key, the Consent Mode defaults and what an answer changes — read by both `nuxt.config.ts` and the banner |
| `shared/utils/savedCharacters.ts` | The remembered character itself: its shape, the key the two lists are deduplicated on, the two limits, and the `localStorage` arms — read by the browser, by an account's own copy and by the endpoints that accept one |
| `shared/utils/wow-{class,faction,professions}.ts` | The names the game's own UI uses, in both languages: the classes, the factions, and the professions (taken from Blizzard's profession index, so a pill reads as the game's profession pane spells it) |
